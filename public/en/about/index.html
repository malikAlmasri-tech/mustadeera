About us

AL-Mustadira started with a call nobody answered.

Booking a pitch usually goes like this: you ask a friend for a number, you call and nobody picks up, you call again and someone says "come at eight", and when you arrive the pitch is taken. Nobody is at fault - the free hours simply live in a paper notebook or in a busy person's memory.

AL-Mustadira moves that notebook somewhere everyone can see at the same time: the player sees what is genuinely free, and the owner sees every request on one screen.

What the platform actually does

For players: browse pitches and their free hours, send a booking request, and follow it through to confirmation.

For owners: a dashboard that collects requests and confirms or declines them with one tap, plus revenue and occupancy reports.

And what it doesn't do

It takes no online payment and stores no cards - there is no payment in the platform at all.

It does not guarantee a booking on the pitch's behalf: confirmation is always the owner's decision, and what you send is a request, not a confirmed booking.

One sport today, built for five

The problem we solve is not specific to football: a padel court and a basketball court get booked through the same unanswered phone call. So the app is built around five sports - football, padel, basketball, tennis and volleyball - and the sport switcher has been in it since day one.

Football alone is open for booking right now, because those are the venues actually registered with us. The other four carry a "Soon" badge instead of invented listings, and we open them one at a time as their venues come in.

One rule we hold to

We show no invented numbers: no ratings written by us, no "thousands of bookings", and no "instant booking" while confirmation genuinely needs the owner. A pitch nobody has rated yet shows no stars - that is deliberate.
