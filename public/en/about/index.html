About us

AL-Mustadira started with a call nobody answered.

Booking a pitch usually means a call nobody answers, then a pitch already taken when you arrive. The fault is that free times live in a paper notebook.

AL-Mustadira moves that notebook somewhere everyone can see at the same time: the player sees what is genuinely free, and the owner sees every request on one screen.

What the platform actually does

For players: browse pitches and their free hours, send a booking request, and follow it through to confirmation.

For owners: a dashboard that collects requests and confirms or declines them with one tap, plus revenue and occupancy reports.

And what it doesn't do

It takes no online payment and stores no cards - there is no payment in the platform at all.

It does not guarantee a booking on the pitch's behalf: confirmation is always the owner's decision, and what you send is a request, not a confirmed booking.

One sport today, built for five

The problem is not specific to football: padel and basketball are booked with the same unanswered call. The app has had five sports from day one.

Football alone is open today because those are the pitches registered with us. We open the rest one at a time as their pitches are registered.

One rule we hold to

No invented numbers: no reviews we write ourselves, and no “instant booking” while the venue is the one who confirms. An unrated venue shows no stars.

What players ask before their first booking

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. The app is Android-only today, and booking happens there and nowhere else. The download page says so plainly rather than wasting your time.

Why does Android warn me during install?

Because the app is downloaded here, not from the Play Store, and Android warns about every app from outside it. The download page has the steps and the file fingerprint.

I own a pitch - how do I register it?

From the For venue owners page: fill in the form and we will call you. Registering is free and open to all five sports.
