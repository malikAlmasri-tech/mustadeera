Installing

Install in three steps

The app is distributed outside the Play Store, so two warnings will stop you. Both are normal, and here is exactly what they say.

Download the file

Tap the download button. Your browser will ask you to confirm because it's an APK - accept.

Allow the install once

Android blocks the first attempt. Tap "Settings" inside that message and allow the browser you downloaded with. You do this once and never again.

"For your security, your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source"

Get past the Play Protect scan

Android may say it could not scan the app - tap “Install anyway”. It says this for every app from outside the store.

"App not scanned" · "Install anyway"

Why does Android warn you at all?

Because the file doesn't come from the Play Store. Google doesn't know it, so it warns by default - the warning is about where the file came from, not what's inside it.

That's why we publish a SHA-256 fingerprint with every release: you can check for yourself that what you downloaded is exactly what we published.

What can the app do on your phone?

One permission: internet. No location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts, no files.

It never asks for a card and never takes a payment - there is no online payment anywhere in the platform.
