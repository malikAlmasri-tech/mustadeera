What players ask before their first booking

Is the app only for football?

No. Five sports are in the app, and football is what is open for booking today because those are the pitches registered with us. We never show a pitch you cannot book.

Can I book from this website?

No. This page only explains what Al-Mustadeera is. Browsing venues and sending requests happen inside the Android app.

Is the app free to use?

Yes. Downloading and using the app are free, and we take no booking fee from you. You pay the hourly price to the pitch owner, at the pitch.

Is my booking confirmed instantly?

No. You send a booking request and the venue confirms it, and you see its status until it is settled. You will never see a taken slot offered as free.

Do I need a bank card?

No. There is no online payment anywhere in the platform and we never ask for card details. Payment happens between you and the pitch.
