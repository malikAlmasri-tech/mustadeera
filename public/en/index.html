Frequent questions

What players ask before their first booking

Is the app only for football?

No. AL-Mustadira is a platform for booking sports venues, and it carries five sports: football, padel, basketball, tennis and volleyball. What is open for booking today is football, because those are the venues registered with us so far; the rest appear in the app with a "Soon" badge. We open them one at a time as their venues are registered, and we never list a venue you cannot book.

Is the app free to use?

Yes. Downloading and using the app are free, and we take no booking fee from you. You pay the hourly price to the pitch owner, at the pitch.

Is my booking confirmed instantly?

No. You send a booking request and the pitch owner confirms it. You can follow the request's status in the app until it is settled - and a booked hour is never shown to you as available.

Do I need a bank card?

No. There is no online payment anywhere in the platform and we never ask for card details. Payment happens between you and the pitch.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. The app is Android-only today, and booking happens there and nowhere else. The download page says so plainly rather than wasting your time.

Why does Android warn me during install?

Because the app is downloaded here rather than from the Play Store, and Android shows that warning for every app installed from outside it. The download page walks through the steps, quotes the warning exactly as it appears on your device, and publishes the file's checksum so you can verify it yourself.

I own a pitch - how do I register it?

Through the For pitch owners page: get in touch and we add your venue, its hours and its prices. Requests then reach you in a dashboard where one tap confirms them. Registering is free, and open to any of the five sports - not football alone.
