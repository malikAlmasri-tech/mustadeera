Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-07-29

This page says exactly what we collect and why, in plain language rather than lawyer's language.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number - because the pitch owner needs both to confirm your booking and reach you.
  • Your password - stored as a one-way encrypted fingerprint (bcrypt). We cannot see it or recover it, and neither can anyone else - which is why there is no "send me my password", only a reset.
  • Your bookings - the pitch, date, time, price and request status.
  • Your ratings and comments, if you write any.

What stays on your device and never reaches us

Favourites, language, dark mode and your session token all live in your browser's storage on your own device. Clearing your browser data erases them.

Who we share it with

  • The pitch owner you sent a request to sees your name, phone number and request details. That is the core of the service, and sending the request is your agreement to it.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers. There are no ads in the platform and no tracking tools on this site.

Artificial intelligence

The owner dashboard has an analysis feature that uses Google Gemini. What gets sent is aggregated numbers about the venue (booking counts, revenue, occupancy, pitch names, weekdays and hours) and review text without the reviewer's name. No player names and no phone numbers are ever sent.

Payment

We collect no payment data because there is no online payment in the platform. We never ask for a card and never store a card number.

Where it is stored

In a managed database (Supabase / PostgreSQL) protected by row-level security, so no account can read another account's data. All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS.

Your rights

  • You can change your name, phone number and password from My Account in the app.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data - we carry it out and confirm it to you.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. On deletion your personal data is removed; booking records may remain de-identified (a figure and a date with no name) for settling accounts with the pitches.

Questions or a deletion request

Write to us from the contact page and we'll reply. Contact
