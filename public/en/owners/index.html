For pitch owners

Your pitch where people look, and your ledger on one screen.

Registering is free, and open to football, padel, basketball, tennis and volleyball venues. You control your hours and prices, and you confirm or decline every request yourself.

What changes for you

The calls stop

Instead of ten scattered calls while you're working, requests arrive in writing in one place, with the time, the pitch and who asked.

No double booking

A confirmed hour disappears from every other player's screen immediately, so there is no clash and no awkward apology.

You know your numbers

Today's and this week's revenue, occupancy rate, busiest hours and your most-booked pitch - all worked out for you.

The decision stays yours

Nothing books itself. Every request waits for your confirmation, and you can decline with a reason.

My venue isn't for football - do you take it?

Yes. AL-Mustadira is built around five sports and the app's switcher shows all of them. Football is open for booking today because those are the venues registered with us; the rest carry a "Soon" badge. We open your sport once its venues are in - and registering today is what brings that closer.

How to register

Get in touch with your venue details: name, sport, area, number of pitches, hours and prices.

We create your account and add your pitch with its photos and amenities.

You sign in to your dashboard and start receiving requests - and change your hours and prices whenever you like.

What does it cost?

Registering your venue and running the dashboard are free. Subscription details are agreed with you before activation.
